Why we built this

Women's health has been under-discussed for too long.

Menopause, periods, pelvic health, heart disease in women, ADHD and autism in women, they're all common, and they've all historically been under-treated or missed because the right questions rarely got asked. The Full Picture exists to ask them properly, on your terms, and to point you toward real help when something's worth a proper conversation.

We're not a replacement for your GP or a diagnosis. We're a clear, honest starting point, checked by a practitioner, built from established medical guidance, and free to take.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Is this a diagnosis?

No. It's an informational starting point built from your answers, reviewed by a practitioner, and designed to make your next conversation with a GP or specialist more useful. It never replaces medical advice.

Is it really free?

Yes, taking the check and getting your report costs nothing.

Do I have to answer every topic?

No. After your life stage, you choose which topics you want covered. You'll only be asked about the ones you pick.

Will a real person see my answers?

Yes. Every report is reviewed by a qualified practitioner. Your account page shows honestly whether that review has happened yet for your report.

What happens to my data?

Your answers are used to build your report. We never sell raw lead data to third parties.

Do I have to join the WhatsApp community?

No, it's entirely optional. You'll get a link after your report is ready if you'd like to join.
